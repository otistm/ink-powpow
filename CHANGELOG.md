# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Shooting is first person now. You look down your own rifle's iron sights, and the view swings round to follow where you aim.
- Drag anywhere to move your sights, let go to fire. A quick tap fires where the sights already are. On a computer, move the mouse and click, or use the arrow keys and space.
- The sights wobble a little, like holding a real rifle up. A dashed ring shows exactly where the cork will land.
- Your rivals sit just off the edges of your view: the backs of their heads and their rifles reaching in, with dashed lines when they fire.
- Soot bombs now land on your view itself, like ink on the lens.

## 0.1.0
- First version. A fairground mechanical shooting gallery. You sit on the middle stool; the shooters either side of you aim at the same tin targets, and whoever hits one first gets the points.
- Tap to shoot. 6 corks a load, reloading by itself or early with a tap. 3 hits in a row doubles your points, 6 triples them; a miss starts you over.
- 29 kinds of target: ducks, rabbits, fish, bottles, cans, teapots, clay pipes, stars, moons, owls, bats, ghosts that fade in and out, crocodiles, rubber chickens, bandits in windows, cactuses, rockets, a swinging bell, balloons, bullseyes and more. Rare ones: the golden goose and a flying saucer. The tin knight takes 2 hits. Mystery boxes give you a shell, wind-up clocks add 3 seconds.
- Black targets cost points: the sheriff, the barker's hat and the booth cat.
- 8 special shells: Ricochet (bounces on to 2 more targets), Buckshot, Firecracker, Skewer, Golden cork (3 times the points), Magnet, Spanner (jams all the machinery) and Soot bomb (your rivals shoot slow and wild). Rivals bring shells too, and some will soot you when they're losing.
- Last call: in the final 10 seconds the machinery and the band organ speed up.
- Walk the midway: 5 booths, each with its own machinery, barker and pair of rivals. The Duck Pond, Tin Can Alley, the Dustbowl Saloon, Moon & Stars, and the Grand Gallery with champion Deadeye Dot. 3 tokens; losing a booth costs one.
- Tickets from every round buy shells and rifle upgrades at the shell cart between booths: Long tube, Quick pump, Steady streak, Deep pockets, Lucky horseshoe, Keen eye.
- Win a booth to put its prize on your shelf: a goldfish in a bag, a tin kazoo, a sheriff's star, a moon balloon and the giant bear.
- Play any booth on its own for a best score.
- Pass the rifle: 2 to 4 friends take turns on one phone. Whoever went before you sits beside you, shooting exactly as they did.
- Sounds made on the fly, rivals' corks pop from their side of you, and a band-organ waltz plays. Both can be turned off.
- Can be added to a phone's home screen like the other Ink games.
